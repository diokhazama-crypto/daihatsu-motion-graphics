# Prompt gambar — Duel di Depan Bank (12 gambar)

Cara pakai:
1. Buka ChatGPT, Gemini, atau AI image generator yang biasa Anda pakai.
2. **Lampirkan character sheet Anda** (foto kemeja navy DAIHATSU) di setiap prompt yang memuat **MAS SALES**, supaya wajah dan bajunya konsisten.
3. Untuk setiap gambar, salin **GAYA** + **KARAKTER** yang muncul + prompt adegannya.
4. Ukuran: **vertikal 9:16** (1080×1920 atau lebih besar).
5. Simpan dengan nama file persis seperti tertulis (`01.png`, `02.png`, …), lalu upload semuanya ke sini.

Bagian bawah gambar (±20%) sebaiknya tidak ada wajah, karena di situ nanti ada subtitle.

---

## GAYA (tempel di awal setiap prompt)

> Vertical 9:16 frame. High-quality 2D animated cartoon style, like a modern animated short: proportional realistic adult humans (not chibi, not big-headed), clean bold black outlines, soft cel shading, expressive faces. Cinematic Wild West night: deep blue and purple moonlight, warm accents, dusty street. No text, no captions, no watermark.

## KARAKTER (tempel yang muncul di adegan)

- **SHERIFF:** a calm, deadpan cowboy sheriff in his 50s, lean, wide cream cowboy hat with brown band, thick droopy brown moustache, a piece of straw in the corner of his mouth, heavy half-closed eyelids, long brown coat over a teal shirt, gold star badge, gun belt with holstered revolver, grey trousers, brown boots.
- **BANDIT:** a nervous young outlaw in his 20s, thin, small black cowboy hat with gold band, stubble, wide frightened eyes, sweat drops, gritted-teeth grin, red western shirt with cream fringe across the chest, yellow neckerchief, black trousers, gun belt with holstered revolver, black boots.
- **MAS SALES:** the man in the attached character sheet (same face, same short black side-parted hair). Wearing his navy short-sleeve DAIHATSU work shirt with white piping on the sleeves and light-blue pattern on the sides, light-blue lanyard with white ID card, beige chinos, navy sneakers. Friendly, confident smile.
- **ROCKY:** a red Daihatsu Rocky compact SUV (2020s model), black lower cladding, silver alloy wheels.
